About

The creative director who still makes things.

Every chapter of my career pairs leading the work with making it. I have led big shoots and held the camera. I have set the brand strategy and built the store. Clients get senior thinking and finished work from the same person.

Dave Paull

Senior brand, creative and communications

I am a creative director based in Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast. For more than 20 years I have led brand, campaign and ecommerce creative for national names like Village Roadshow and Goodman Fielder, and for founders who needed to raise money or sell product fast.

I still lead and make the work myself: strategy, identity, photography, packaging and online stores, with specialists brought in when a job needs them. Before all of this I grew up in a family that has worked in food for three generations, from the markets to retail, and then worked in tourism. That is where I learned how things actually get sold.
