What people ask before they call.

How much does it cost?

Every job gets a fixed quote before any work starts, so there are no hourly surprises. Prices are not listed because the work ranges from a single shoot to a full rebuild. The work is scoped to the problem, not to an agency’s overhead. If what you need is out of reach I will say so on the call, and tell you what I would do first instead.

Who do you work with?

Founders and established businesses with something valuable that isn’t being understood: a better product, a new technology, a raise or a relaunch. Size matters less than whether the message is what is holding you back.

Who actually does the work?

I do. One senior creative, with a trusted specialist network for the parts that need more hands. You deal with me directly and I keep creative responsibility from the first call to handover. I only take on what I can deliver properly, and on the call I will tell you my next available start date.

Do I need a whole new website to book a shoot?

No. A shoot, a logo, a video or a single page can be a job on its own. I will still ask where the work is going to be used, because that changes how I make it.

Who owns the work when it is finished?

You do. Photographs, design files, website code and logins are handed over when the job is paid. No lock in.

Where are you, and do you travel?

Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast. I shoot on location across South East Queensland and further by arrangement, and work with clients elsewhere in Australia and overseas by video call.

Do you use AI?

Yes, where it makes the work faster or better: research, first drafts, prototypes and simple tools. Everything that leaves the studio has been checked and finished by a person, and your information stays private.
