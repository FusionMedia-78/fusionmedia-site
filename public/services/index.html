Ways to work together

A project, a retainer, or a part time creative director.

Project

One job with a start, an end and a fixed quote agreed before anything begins. A shoot, a rebrand, a store, a pitch deck.

Retainer

A set amount of my time each month for the businesses that always have something on: campaigns, new pages, photography, email.

Part time creative director

Sometimes called a fractional creative director. A senior creative lead inside your business for a day or two a week. I set the direction, brief your team or suppliers, and make the pieces that matter most.

No price list, because jobs range from one shoot to a full rebuild. Every job gets a fixed quote before work starts, scoped to the problem rather than to an agency’s overhead.

How a job runs

Five steps, first call to handover.

  1. 1

    A 20 minute clarity call

    You tell me what you’re trying to sell, who needs to understand it, and where the message breaks down. I tell you what I would do first, even if that is not me.

  2. 2

    A fixed quote and a plan

    What I will make, in what order, by when, and what it costs. Agreed before anything starts.

  3. 3

    Message first

    Before I design or shoot anything I check what it needs to say and where it will be used. On a single shoot that is a quick conversation, not a strategy project.

  4. 4

    I make it

    The shoot, the design, the build. You see work in progress, not a big reveal at the end.

  5. 5

    Everything handed over

    Photographs, design files, code and logins are yours when the job is paid. No lock in.

Tools, and where AI fits

I use AI the way I use any good tool: for research, first drafts and prototypes, where it makes the work faster or better. Everything that leaves the studio has been checked and finished by me, and your information stays private.

Tools I work in: Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Shopify, Braze, Emailify, Mailchimp, Monday.com, and Astro, Tailwind, GitHub and Netlify for websites. If you use something else, tell me on the call.
