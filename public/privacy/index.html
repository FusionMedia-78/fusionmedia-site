Privacy

Privacy policy

What happens to the details you send through this website, in plain English.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Fusion Media Creative Agency Pty Ltd (“Fusion Media”, “we”, “us”), PO Box 3180, Burleigh Town QLD 4220. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Enquiry form: your name, email, phone (optional), the kind of business you run, your website (optional) and your message.
  • Free website check: the web address you ask us to check. If you ask for the full report, we also collect your name, email and phone (optional), along with the address checked, its grade and a link to the report.
  • Email and phone: whatever you include when you contact us directly.

This site does not use advertising trackers, analytics or cookies that follow you around the web.

How the website check works

When you enter a web address, our server looks at publicly available information about that website, such as its security settings, public pages and public DNS records, the same information any visitor or search engine can see. Results are kept briefly, about 10 minutes, so repeat checks are quick. To stop misuse, we temporarily limit how many checks come from the same internet address; that limit is not stored as a record of you.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry or send the report you asked for.
  • To follow up about the work you asked about.
  • To keep normal business records.

We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be added.

Where your details are stored

This website is hosted by Netlify, and form submissions are stored in our Netlify account. Netlify is based in the United States, so your details may be stored or processed overseas. We receive form notifications and email through Google Workspace. Both providers have their own security and privacy commitments. We only share your details with other people when you have asked us to, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep them

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your request and to meet our business record-keeping obligations, then delete them.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted. Email dave@fusionmedia.wtf and we will respond within 30 days.

Questions or complaints

Contact Dave Paull at dave@fusionmedia.wtf or 0402 034 986. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, for example by adding website analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top.
