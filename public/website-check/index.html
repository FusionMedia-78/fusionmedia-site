    What the check looks at

    Five things that decide whether people, and AI, trust your site.

    Website safety

    Whether visitors’ browsers trust your site, or warn them off before it loads.

    Email protection

    Whether someone could send emails that look like they come from your business.

    Found by AI assistants

    Whether tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity are let in to read your site.

    Understood by Google & AI

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The quick check is free, and so is the full report. If you’d like help fixing anything, you get a fixed quote first.

    Do you keep my website details?

    The quick check doesn’t save anything. If you ask for the full report, your name, email and website come to me so I can send it. Nobody else sees them.

    My grade is low. Is my site broken?

    Not necessarily. Most small business websites have a few of these gaps, usually because nobody told the person who built it to look. Most are quick to put right once you know exactly what they are.

    Can you check a competitor’s site?

    The check works on any public website. Comparing yours with a competitor’s is a good way to see where you stand.
